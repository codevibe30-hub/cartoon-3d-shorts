# Sumber fakta — Sewa Pacar Imlek (v2 #22, LUAR)

Topik: "Di China, Pacar Bisa Disewa Rp22 Juta Sehari Buat Imlek?!"
Riset: 27 Nov 2026.

## Fakta terverifikasi

1. **Tarif puncak: sampai 10.000 yuan/hari saat Imlek** (aplikasi Hire Me Plz; People's Daily 2017: "prices can surge to up to 10,000 yuan (£1,154) a day" selama Tahun Baru Imlek).
   - http://en.people.cn/n3/2017/0127/c90000-9172316.html
   - Reuters via BusinessDay: "thousands of attractive twenty-somethings like Luoluo commanding fees of 3,000 yuan to 10,000 yuan a day" saat Imlek. https://clone.businessday.ng/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/182448

2. **10.000 yuan ≈ Rp22 juta** (kurs ±2.200 IDR/yuan). Dipakai sebagai padanan rupiah di naskah.

3. **300+ toko sewa pacar di Taobao** (AFP, 1 Feb 2013: "More than 300 results appeared on the popular shopping website Taobao.com to rent a pretend partner ahead of the Lunar New Year holiday").
   - https://terradaily.com/mr-right-for-rent-in-china-999/
   - AEI: "there are more than 300 boyfriend-rental services currently listed on the Chinese shopping website Taobao". https://www.aei.org/economics/markets-in-everything-asian-edition/

4. **Menu harga per aksi** (eChinaCities, terjemahan ifeng.com; dikonfirmasi AFP 2013):
   - Ngobrol: 30 yuan/jam ("Walking down the street and watching a movie together will set you back 30 RMB an hour"; AFP: "Chatting: 30 yuan ($5) an hour").
   - Makan bareng: 50 yuan/jam ("Dining together? 50 RMB an hour").
   - Ciuman: 50 yuan sekali ("for the much needed deal-breaker, the kiss, expect to pay 50 RMB a pop").
   - https://www.echinacities.com/china-news/Rent-a-Boyfriend-Online-The-New-Spring-Festival-Sensation

5. **Kontrak sewa pacar TIDAK SAH di mata hukum** (People's Daily 2017: "the practice is not protected by law in China... some lawyers said the document is often invalid because China's contract law defines subject matters as goods or objects and people cannot be recognized as rental objects").
   - http://en.people.cn/n3/2017/0119/c90000-9168767.html
   - Dimsum Daily, 18 Feb 2026: "contracts based on 'personal lease' have no legal standing whatsoever, as individuals cannot be treated as rental commodities." https://www.dimsumdaily.hk/legal-experts-warn-of-perils-as-young-chinese-increasingly-look-to-rent-a-date-services-for-lunar-new-year-pressure/

6. **Taobao sudah melarangnya** (Culture Yard: "As of January 2022, Taobao no longer displays results for boyfriend rental in China").
   - https://www.cultureyard.net/blog/rent-boyfriend-in-china

7. **Motifnya: tekanan menikah saat pulang kampung Imlek** — orang tua/desakan keluarga menanyakan jodoh; jasa ini jadi "buffer" supaya tidak diributkan soal pernikahan (eChinaCities, People's Daily, China Odyssey Tours).
   - https://www.chinaodysseytours.com/inspiration/chinese-new-year-facts.html

8. **Hire Me Plz: 700.000 pengguna, 1,7 juta pengikut WeChat** (People's Daily 2017; sejak launching 2015).
   - http://en.people.cn/n3/2017/0127/c90000-9172316.html
