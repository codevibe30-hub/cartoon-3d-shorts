# Sumber fakta — Dubai ATM Emas "Gold to Go" (v2 #56, LUAR)

Diverifikasi 26 Nov 2026 dari pencarian web (Guinness World Records, New Atlas, The Street, Breaking Travel News, Gold Bars Worldwide/Ex Oriente Lux, Gulf Buzz):

1. **Gold to Go** — Mesin penjual emas otomatis ("gold ATM") buatan perusahaan Jerman Ex Oriente Lux AG (TG Gold-Super-Markt), penemu Thomas Geissler. Diluncurkan Mei 2010. (Guinness World Records "First ATM to dispense gold bars"; New Atlas 13 Mei 2010.)
2. **Mesin publik pertama di dunia: Emirates Palace Hotel, Abu Dhabi** — bukan Dubai. Guinness: "the first publicly available gold ATM was located in the Emirates Palace Hotel in Abu Dhabi". Mesin Dubai menyusul di Atlantis The Palm, Souk Madinat Jumeirah, Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall. (Guinness; Gulf Buzz; Beno.)
3. **Bodi mesin dilapisi daun emas (gold-leaf finish)** — "A gold machine should be made of gold" (Thomas Geissler). (Breaking Travel News; The Street.)
4. **Produk: batangan emas 24 karat 1 g, 5 g, 10 g** (sampai 1 ons di beberapa mesin) + **koin emas** bergambar Krugerrand (Afrika Selatan), maple leaf (Kanada), kanguru (Australia). (Guinness; New Atlas; Professional Jeweller.)
5. **Harga diperbarui tiap 10 menit** mengikuti harga emas dunia real-time (feed harga diperbarui tiap 10 detik, tarif mesin dikunci per 10 menit). (The Street; Breaking Travel News; New Atlas.)
6. **Hari pertama ludes** — Pada debut Emirates Palace, stok awal ±100 keping habis dalam sehari; dua orang masing-masing memborong ±AED 20.000. (Gulf Buzz.)
7. **Seberat kendaraan lapis baja** — Inventor: mesin "outfitted like an armored vehicle"; bodi baja diperkuat, berat ±500 kg, tahan serangan. (The Street/NYT via Gulf Buzz.)
8. **Anti pencucian uang** — Satu pengguna maks 3x akses per 24 jam lalu wajib jeda 48 jam; pembelian di atas €1.000 wajib scan identitas. (New Atlas.) Naskah menyederhanakan jadi "cuma boleh beli tiga kali sehari".
9. **Dubai = City of Gold** — Julukan lama Dubai karena perdagangan emas fisik (Gold Souk Deira). (DXB Talk.)
