# Sumber fakta — China Gaokao (v2 #25, LUAR)

Topik: "Di China, 12,9 Juta Siswa Ikut Ujian yang Bikin Satu Negara Berhenti"
Riset: 27 Nov 2026.

## Fakta terverifikasi

1. **Peserta 2026: 12,9 juta siswa** (angka terbaru, diumumkan Kementerian Pendidikan China 3 Juni 2026, dilaporkan China Daily / The Star). Turun 450.000 dari 2025 — penurunan kedua berturut-turut.
   - Rise Asia: tabel peserta — 2024: 13,42 juta (rekor); 2025: 13,35 juta; 2026: 12,9 juta. https://riseasia.co/what-is-gaokao/
   - The Homework Site (mengutip Kemendik via gov.cn + China News Service): "China's gaokao drew about 12.9 million registered candidates in 2026". https://thehomeworksite.com/research/gaokao-vs-sat/

2. **Rekor 2024: 13,42 juta peserta** — tertinggi sejak ujian dipulihkan 1977. 2025: 13,35 juta (turun 70.000, penurunan pertama dalam 8 tahun).
   - British Council: "Around 13.35 million people took part in China's national college entrance exam in June 2025... In 2024, Gaokao participation hit a record 13.42 million." https://opportunities-insight.britishcouncil.org/short-articles/news/slight-dip-gaokao-candidates-after-2024s-record-high
   - Xinhua via gov.cn (28 Mei 2025): 13,35 juta siswa akan ikut Gaokao mulai 7 Juni. https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202505/28/content_WS6836ac07c6d0868f4e8f2edf.html

3. **Jadwal: 7–10 Juni tiap tahun** (4 hari; Beijing, Tianjin, Zhejiang, Shandong sampai 10 Juni). Ujian masuk perguruan tinggi nasional — penentu utama masa depan siswa.
   - TravelChinaWithMe; The Times ("young people... feel their future depends on just four days of struggle").

4. **Soal ujian = rahasia negara**: dicetak di fasilitas ultra-aman / penjara khusus, diawasi CCTV 24 jam, diangkut dengan kawalan polisi bersenjata + pelacakan GPS + perekaman video.
   - Bhaskar English (mengutip SCMP + studi Ohio State University): "Gaokao question papers are classified as 'state secrets'... printed in ultra-secure facilities or specially designated prisons... transported under armed police escort with GPS tracking, video monitoring." https://www.bhaskarenglish.in/national/news/neet-2026-leak-exam-spark-security-debate-china-ai-drones-armed-escorts-signal-jammers-stop-cheating-137956654.html

5. **Pengamanan anti-curang**: detektor logam + gerbang keamanan pintar, pengenalan wajah / sidik jari / iris, jammer sinyal radio di sekitar lokasi, drone + tim SWAT memantau area ujian.
   - Bhaskar English (mengutip China Daily + Xinhua). SeaPRwire: "Shandong implemented full-process examination paper tracking, including Beidou positioning systems, police escorts, video recording, and around-the-clock monitoring."

6. **Negara berhenti**: jalan di sekitar sekolah ditutup / larangan membunyikan klakson, konstruksi dihentikan, pembatasan penerbangan di dekat lokasi ujian, transportasi umum diprioritaskan untuk siswa, hotel dekat sekolah diawasi harganya.
   - The Times: "The road outside Chen Jinglun High School in Beijing was sealed off... Traffic was barred. A sign demanded no honking of horns."
   - SeaPRwire: "Cities across China activated noise-control programs... Construction work and other noise-producing activities near testing centers faced restrictions... Police departments opened expedited identification services."

7. **Orang tua menunggu di luar sekolah**: berdoa, membawa makanan, menunggu anaknya keluar saat istirahat — digambarkan Reuters ("Prayers before China's 'gaokao' exam season", "Parents wait behind police cordon outside exam venue").
   - DevDiscourse: "Parents nationwide gathered outside exam centers, offering moral support and snacks, while police deployed drones."

## Angka yang dipakai di naskah
- Peserta: **12,9 juta** (2026, terbaru — klaim hook).
- Rekor: 13,42 juta (2024) — disebut di sumber, tidak di naskah (naskah pakai angka terbaru).
- Durasi ujian: **empat hari** (7–10 Juni).
- Soal = rahasia negara, dicetak di penjara, kawalan polisi bersenjata.
- Jammer sinyal, drone, jalan ditutup, konstruksi berhenti.
- Orang tua berdoa di luar sekolah.
